Services

  • TPI golf assessment

    A full movement screen that shows which physical limits are affecting your swing, and what to do about them.

  • Golf injury rehabilitation

    Treatment for pain in the low back, hips, shoulders, elbows, wrists and knees, with a plan to get you back on the course.

  • Swing-related pain evaluation

    If golf hurts, we find out why and treat the cause, not just the symptom.

  • Mobility, strength and stability programs

    Exercises matched to your assessment results so the work carries over to your swing.

Background

Swinglab Rehab is led by a physical therapist with more than 20 years of clinical experience. He combines hands-on rehabilitation with the golf-specific testing developed by the Titleist Performance Institute (TPI).

Whether you are recovering from an injury or want to play without pain, every plan starts with how you move and what your swing asks of your body.
